# Project Brief

## Purpose
Free web app (PWA) for joining pickup football matches with strangers in Ho Chi Minh City and splitting the pitch fee. Personal-brand project by Long; no revenue goal.

## MVP Scope
Sign in (Google, Zalo) · create match · share link with preview · nearby match list · take a slot on team A/B · pay host via VietQR (outside the app) · host confirms payment and attendance · simple player profile. Details: `spec.md`.

## Non-goals
Money handling, chat, push notifications, native apps, court booking, tournaments, other sports, monetization.

## Success Definition
- Real matches played in District 2 that filled slots through the app.
- People who use or hear about the app can find out Long built it.
- A clean public (MIT) codebase that demonstrates Rust backend craft.
- Starts at ~100 users and scales by configuration, not rewrites.
