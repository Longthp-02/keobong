# Product Context

## Why It Exists
Pickup matchmaking in Vietnam happens in Facebook/Zalo groups with no slot tracking, no payment tracking and frequent no-shows. Abroad, apps such as LaBOLA (Japan, individual futsal sign-ups), Footy Addicts (UK) and Playtomic (open matches with visible team slots) solve this. Local apps (Sporta, Alobo) focus on venue booking; Phui focuses on managing an existing team. The individual-player slot is the gap.

## Target Users
Players looking for a game, hosts with open slots, and later venues with idle hours.

## Main Workflows
Create match → share link → join slot → pay host → host confirms → play → host marks attendance. Browse nearby matches.

## Business / Domain Goals
Free forever, minimal running cost, trust between strangers (payment proof, attendance record), viral spread through share links in Zalo groups.
