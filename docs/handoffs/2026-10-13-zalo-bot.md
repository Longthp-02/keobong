# Handoff: Zalo bot linking (2026-10-13)

## Endpoints
- `POST /api/bot/zalo/webhook`: Zalo only (header `X-Bot-Api-Secret-Token`). 200 `{ "ok": true }` for any body once the secret is right (the body is parsed after the check); 403 with a missing or wrong secret; 404 when the bot is not configured.
- `POST /api/bot/link`, signed in, body `{ "code": "<43 URL-safe characters>" }`: 200 `{ "linked": true }`; 410 `invalid_code` (unknown, used or older than 15 minutes); 400 `invalid_request`; 401; 404 `bot_unavailable`. `Cache-Control: private, no-store`.

## Frontend
`/bot/link?code=…` checks the code's shape, asks a signed-out visitor to sign in (returning to the same URL), then shows `BotLinkForm`: linking needs a click and names the account it links. `browserLinkZalo` maps the statuses.
